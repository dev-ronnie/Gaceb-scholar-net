OUR STORY

Learning with purpose

GACEB began in 2025, inspired by people with potential and the belief that learning opportunities should help every student grow.

GACEB Scholar is designed for Uganda's O-Level and A-Level learners, bringing learning resources, student contributions and study support together in one accessible place.
